📖 HOW TO PLAY

  • 👆➜Drag your finger from one yellow dot to another to run a cable.
  • 👆〰Tap a cable to remove it.
  • 👆🔘Tap a switch to flip it ON / OFF.
  • ⌨️Type your name with the on-screen keyboard before your turn.
  • 💥Never connect + straight to − — the bulbs explode and you lose precious seconds ⏰.
  • 🖱️➜Click and hold a yellow dot, drag to another dot, release to connect.
  • 🖱️〰Click a cable to remove it.
  • 🖱️🔘Click a switch to flip it ON / OFF.
  • ⌨️Type your name with the keyboard or the on-screen keys.
  • 💥Never connect + straight to − — the bulbs explode and you lose precious seconds ⏰.
  • 🕹️Left stick / D-pad moves the glove cursor 🧤.
  • ⒶPress A on a dot to start a cable, move to another dot and press A again to connect.
  • ⒶPress A on a switch to flip it, on a cable to remove it, on a button to select it.
  • ⒷB cancels the cable you are holding.
  • ⓎY clears all cables.   START begins a turn.
  • 🎮Plug in any USB / Bluetooth controller (Xbox, PlayStation, generic) and press a button — it connects automatically.
